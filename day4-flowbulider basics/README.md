->What is Flow Builder?
  Flow Builder is a no-code automation tool in Salesforce used to automate business processes and workflows

 ->Types of Flows

* Screen Flow
  Screen Flow allows users to enter information through screens and forms.
  Example:
  Student registration form.

 *  Record Triggered Flow
   Record Triggered Flow runs automatically when a record is created, updated, or deleted.
  Example:
  Send confirmation email after student registration.

---

## Automation Ideas for College Management System

1. Auto email after student registration
2. Generate student ID automatically
3. Notify faculty when course is full
4. Send reminder before fee deadline
5. Update remaining seats automatically

---

## Flow Design Thinking

### Automation Process:
Auto Email After Registration

### Trigger:
Student record created

### Steps:
1. Check student email
2. Generate confirmation message
3. Send email automatically

### Final Action:
Student receives confirmation email.

---

## Manual vs Automated Process

### Manual Process
Staff manually sends fee reminders to students.

### Problems
- Time consuming
- Human errors
- Some students may be missed

### Automated Process
Salesforce automatically sends reminders before fee deadlines.

---

## Reflection

Companies automate repetitive business processes to save time, reduce errors, improve consistency, and increase productivity.
